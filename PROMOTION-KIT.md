# FormFix - Week 1 Promotion Kit

## Product Tagline (for all platforms)
Free browser-based image & PDF tools — no uploads, no accounts, 100% private.

## Short Description (50 words)
FormFix is a free suite of 30+ browser-based tools for images, PDFs, and more. Compress images to exact KB, create passport photos, merge PDFs, generate QR codes, and much more. Everything runs locally in your browser — your files never leave your device. No sign-up required.

## Medium Description (150 words)
FormFix is a free, privacy-first toolkit with 30+ browser-based tools for images, PDFs, and everyday tasks. Unlike cloud-based alternatives, FormFix processes everything locally in your browser — your files are never uploaded to any server.

Key tools include:
- Image Compressor: Compress to exact file sizes (20KB, 50KB, 100KB)
- Passport Photo: Create official 3.5x4.5cm photos with white/grey backgrounds
- PDF Merger/Splitter/Rotator: Combine, split, or rotate PDFs offline
- Signature Maker: Draw or type signatures with 65+ professional fonts
- Text to Handwriting: Convert typed text into realistic handwritten assignments
- QR Code Generator: Create branded QR codes instantly
- Resume Builder: Build professional CVs in under a minute
- Background Remover: AI-powered background removal
- Image format converter, resizer, cropper, watermark tool, and more

All tools are 100% free with no file size limits, no watermarks, and no registration required.
